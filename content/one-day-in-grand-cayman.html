Classic Grand Cayman Port Day

  1. 08:00
    Depart pier

    Meet Stingray City boat at marina — morning slots have calmer North Sound.

  2. 09:30
    Stingray City sandbar

    Waist-deep turquoise water with southern stingrays.

  3. 11:30
    Barrier reef snorkel

    Second stop on many combo tours — coral and tropical fish.

  4. 14:00
    Seven Mile Beach or Starfish Point

    If time allows — otherwise return early with buffer.

  5. 16:30
    Back at pier

    Build your own buffer before published all-aboard — confirm times with your ship and operator.
