How long do cruise ships stay in Grand Cayman?

Most calls are 7 to 10 hours. Stingray City combos take roughly 3–4 hours; adding Seven Mile Beach needs an early start and a late all-aboard.

Is Stingray City safe for children?

Yes, with crew supervision on organised tours. Water is shallow; follow briefing so kids interact gently with stingrays.

Do I need Cayman Islands cash?

US dollars are widely accepted. Small vendors may prefer cash; ATMs are in George Town near the port.

Tender port or dock?

Depends on the ship and pier assignment. Check your daily programme — add tender time when booking morning Stingray departures.

Ship excursion or book independently?

Ship-sold tours often include a wait-if-late policy from the cruise line. Independent operators typically plan a return window — confirm policies, build your own buffer, and do not cut it fine.
