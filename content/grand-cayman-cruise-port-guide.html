Cruise ships call at George Town on Grand Cayman. Many visits involve tendering ashore, but landing arrangements vary by ship, pier capacity and the day — always confirm with your cruise line. Typical calls run 7–10 hours.

Where ships arrive

George Town waterfront

Downtown shops, taxis and excursion meeting points concentrate near the harbour. Confirm your operator meeting point — multi-ship days get busy.

Tender logistics

When your ship tenders, budget queue and boat time both ways. That is especially important before morning Stingray departures. See the tender planning guide for a careful checklist.

Currency

Cayman Islands dollar (KYD); US dollars widely accepted at excursions and taxis.

Language

English — straightforward for UK and North American cruise guests.

Getting around

Licensed taxis at the landing; many Stingray and reef tours include boat transfer from marinas near town.
